# Editorial profiles

Use these as starting points, not presets that overrule the footage.

## Talking head

- Lead with the clearest promise or consequence.
- Remove dead air and fillers when removal improves delivery; preserve intentional breaths and emotional pauses.
- Hide distracting jump cuts with modest reframing, supplied B-roll, screenshots, or graphic callouts.
- Prefer continuous room tone and natural speech over maximum compression.

## App demo

- Organize around the user outcome, not the order in which screens were recorded.
- Keep UI text readable. Avoid crops that remove navigation context or touch targets.
- Use zooms/callouts only to direct attention at the moment of interaction.
- Match narration to the visible app state; never show a completed action before its spoken setup without intent.

## Short-form

- Put the strongest concrete hook in the opening seconds.
- Remove throat-clearing, redundant setup, and repeated conclusions.
- Use 9:16 when the destination is TikTok/Reels/Shorts; verify face and UI reframing shot by shot.
- Keep captions continuously useful, but do not cover faces, controls, or platform chrome safe areas.
- Avoid generic high-energy effects that make personal footage feel like an ad template.

## Interview

- Preserve speaker intent, reactions, conversational rhythm, and identifiers.
- Use diarization and reaction frames; do not cut so tightly that answers feel fabricated.
- Reordering topics is allowed when disclosed. Splicing separate phrases into a new claim is not.
- Normalize speakers without flattening natural dynamics.

## General

- Infer the viewer promise and build the shortest coherent structure that fulfills it.
- Prefer motivated cuts: new information, action, emotion, perspective, or time.
- Use references by measuring traits such as average shot length, caption density, framing, palette, transition frequency, and sound design. Do not copy distinctive creative assets.

## Default captions

- Always include captions for speech.
- Use sentence-aware phrases, normally 2–7 words per card.
- Highlight at most one meaningful phrase; do not animate every word by default.
- Use high contrast, safe margins, and a stable baseline.
- Correct product names and domain terms before rendering.
