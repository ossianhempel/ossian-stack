# Independent reviews

Read when comparing revisions or when an audit needs judgment independent of
the conversation that developed the piece. A new agent is useful only if its
context is fresh; a reviewer inheriting the drafting conversation is not an
independent reader.

## Give the reviewer enough to judge

Use the host's native subagent facility with conversation inheritance disabled,
or a fresh session with equivalent isolation. Supply only:

- The complete text, or both versions labeled A and B in an arbitrary order.
- The intended reader, platform, purpose, and known evidence or factual limits.
- Relevant voice constraints and necessary surrounding text. If the reader
  contract is itself under review, ask the reviewer to infer it rather than
  giving away what the author intended readers to understand.
- A bounded task and the diagnostic-only output boundary below.

Omit prior critique, revision chronology, which version is original, who wrote
which version, the parent's verdict, and the answer the author hopes to hear.
Use neutral filenames or include the text directly if paths reveal this history.
Keep necessary factual constraints even when they weaken an option.

## Bound the review

One reviewer is usually enough. Ask for the specific judgment needed: a reader's
understanding, the evidence supporting a claim, a focused clarity pass, or a
comparison against the reader contract. Avoid generic requests to improve the
piece and don't ask for a praise section.

For an independent read, request what the reviewer understood the main point and
payoff to be, where understanding or attention broke down, and the passages
supporting that diagnosis. Don't turn the author's intended meaning into a hint.

For a comparison, request a preference, mixed result, or no meaningful difference;
concrete gains and losses with passage evidence; and any credibility or voice
tradeoff. A smoother or shorter version need not win if it loses meaning,
specificity, or the writer's character. Don't generate a third version.

The reviewer should identify locations, reader effects, and revision operations,
without replacement prose, source edits, further delegation, or publication.
Give the reviewer the applicable instructions directly or the absolute path of
the skill you loaded; don't rely on it inheriting this conversation or finding
an installed copy of the same revision.

## Use the result honestly

Check findings against the text and known evidence. Preserve meaningful
disagreement rather than forcing consensus or overruling a result because the
preferred revision lost. Explain relevant tradeoffs and uncertainty to the
writer; don't present a reviewer vote as proof of audience performance.

If the result turns on version order or the decision warrants another check,
use another fresh context with labels swapped. Reusing the same reviewer after
revealing the history does not restore independence.

When a fresh context is unavailable, disclose that limitation. Offer a comparison
or second pass in the current context without calling it blind or independent.
Still provide useful diagnostic findings and leave the final choice to the writer.
