# In your words

The user wants to check that you understood them before any work happens. Play
back their last request, and the intent behind it, in your own words. Paraphrase
it, so a misreading shows. Echoing their phrasing back would hide one.

Cover, in a few short lines:

- **Goal:** what they want to end up with, and why, as you read it.
- **Scope:** what is in, and anything you take to be out.
- **Assumptions:** what you are filling in that they did not say. If two
  readings are plausible, name both and say which one you would go with.

Stop there. Take no action on the request, and give no plan unless they asked
for one. End with one line asking whether that is right. Once they confirm or
correct you, carry on with the corrected reading.
